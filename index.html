I earned my Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, where I worked in romi-lab under the supervision of Prof. David Navarro-Alarcon. I was also a visiting student at the National University of Singapore, supervised by Prof. Guillaume Sartoretti.

My research explores how robots can reason, plan, and learn to act in the physical world. I work on long-horizon planning, vision-language-action models, and world models, drawing on reinforcement learning and imitation learning.
