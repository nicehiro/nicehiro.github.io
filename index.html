Research

My research focuses on robotics and deep reinforcement learning. My recent works explore:

  • Embodied visual reasoning for VLA manipulation (arXiv'26)
  • Instruction-augmented long-horizon planning for mobile manipulation (AAAI'25)
  • Subgoal planning for long-horizon tasks with sparse rewards (TASE'25)
  • Cross-embodiment humanoid robot control (RLJ'25)
